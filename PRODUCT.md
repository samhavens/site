# Personal site

## Register

brand

## Users and purpose

Readers of Sam Havens's existing professional site, including people following a link directly to an essay. The blog extends the plain, static personal site. Readers should be able to read the argument, change assumptions, and inspect the code without installing anything.

## Established direction

The reference is the live samuelhavens.com homepage and this repository's raw HTML/CSS. Sam explicitly requested that the new blog match that site. Use its system typography, light background, narrow reading column, simple links, and understated rules. The recovered essay supplies the explanatory voice: informal, mostly lowercase, concrete about assumptions. Sam rejected dramatic ancestry aphorisms throughout the earlier version.

## Principles

- Let the reasoning and working model carry the page.
- Preserve the site's identity rather than introducing a separate publication brand.
- Keep assumptions and definitions near their outputs.
- Give readers a useful first comparison and expandable detail.
- Keep a readable static document with source links.

## Accessibility

Preserve the recovered draft's labeled controls, keyboard operation, text alternatives, reduced-motion support, and no-JavaScript reading path. Do not require animation to understand a result. Use adequate contrast and local horizontal scrolling for wide tables.

## Scope

Prepare a local real-name draft. Publication follows Sam's review. No analytics, external runtime dependencies, or personal genealogy data are needed.
