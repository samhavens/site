# Existing site design

Reference inspected: index.html and https://www.samuelhavens.com/.

The homepage uses system-ui, 16px body text, line-height 1.55, a maximum width of 860px, 16px side padding, a 34px bold title, uppercase 18px section labels, inherited-color underlined links, and thin neutral rules. Its background is light and it has no decorative imagery, cards, navigation shell, or entrance animation.

The blog keeps those choices. Long-form article text uses a slightly narrower measure. Interactive diagrams may extend beyond the reading column, with a full-width mobile layout. Use a restrained palette for the surrounding document and named colors only for the quantities or groups in the model. Preserve the system font rather than introducing a new display typeface.

Readers are following a professional-site link and reading an essay on a laptop or phone in ordinary daylight. A light document matches both that setting and the existing site. Motion belongs inside the explanatory diagrams, controlled by the reader, with a static equivalent and prefers-reduced-motion support.
